Who We Are

Creating hope through compassion, service and humanity.

Compassion Is Our Identity

Feed And Forest Legal Aid was established with a vision to build a society where no animal remains hungry, every child receives education, nature is protected, women are empowered and every individual gets equal justice.

Through volunteers, donors and community support, our organization continues to create positive impact every single day.

We believe that even the smallest act of kindness can change someone's entire life.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate society where every human, every animal and every tree receives love, care, protection and equal opportunities.

Our Mission

To serve society through animal welfare, tree plantation, child education, women empowerment, legal awareness and humanitarian services.

Our Values

Compassion, Transparency, Integrity, Equality, Service and Dedication guide every initiative we undertake.

Our Core Objectives

Together we work to create a happier, healthier and greener future for everyone.

Animal Welfare

Rescuing, feeding and providing medical care to stray and abandoned animals with compassion.

Tree Plantation

Building a greener future through plantation drives and environmental awareness campaigns.

Child Education

Providing educational support, books and learning opportunities for underprivileged children.

Women Empowerment

Supporting women's dignity, health and self-reliance through various awareness programmes.

Community Kitchen

Serving nutritious meals to people facing hunger and financial hardships.

Elderly Care

Providing food, medical assistance, care and emotional support to senior citizens.

Our Commitment

At Feed And Forest Legal Aid, we believe every act of kindness creates hope. We are committed to serving society with complete transparency, honesty and dedication.

Our volunteers and supporters work tirelessly to feed hungry animals, plant trees, educate children, empower women and provide legal awareness to those who need it most.
