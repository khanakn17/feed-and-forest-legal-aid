Our Work

Creating positive change through service, compassion and awareness.

🐕 Animal Welfare

Providing food, care and support for stray animals. Working towards a safer and kinder environment for animals.

📚 Child Education

Supporting children's education and helping them get better learning opportunities.

🌱 Environment Conservation

Organising tree plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns and environmental awareness activities to protect nature for future generations.

🍛 Langar & Food Distribution

Serving fresh and nutritious meals to poor families,homeless people, patients and anyone facing hunger or hardship.

🛕 Pilgrimage Support

Providing food, water, medical assistance and guidance to elderly and needy pilgrims during religious journeys and events.

🚑 Disaster Relief

Delivering emergency food, medicines, clothing and essential supplies to families affected by floods, fires and other disasters.

👩 Women Empowerment

Supporting women through skill development, education, legal awareness and opportunities for financial independence.

🩺 Health & Medical Camps

Conducting free health check-ups, medical camps, hygiene awareness and medicine distribution in underserved communities.

⚖️ Legal Aid And Women Support

Spreading legal awareness and supporting women through necessary resources and guidance.
